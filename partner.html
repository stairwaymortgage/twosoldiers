The opportunity

A platform that has never existed.

TwoSoldiers.org is a live video service connecting veterans from opposing sides of past and present conflicts — in real time, across 100+ languages, with AI translation. Two men. One screen. No agenda. Just the truth.

"The reason is always manufactured by someone who will never bleed."

AI translation has made this technically possible. The will to build it is what has been missing. TwoSoldiers.org is that will — made into a platform.

The roadmap

From founding to platform.

  • 01

    Foundation — Now

    Building the email list, validating demand through the YES/NO vote, establishing founding partnerships, and securing initial funding for platform development.

  • 02

    Build — Year One

    Developing platform infrastructure, integrating AI translation across the initial language set, building moderation protocols, and onboarding first veteran organization partners.

  • 03

    Pilot — Year Two

    First live sessions between American Vietnam veterans and Vietnamese veterans on all sides — NVA, Viet Cong, and ARVN. Documented with permission. The proof of concept that shows the world what happens when two soldiers finally find each other.

  • 04

    Scale — Year Three and Beyond

    100+ languages. Global veteran organization partnerships. Documentary series. The most significant veteran healing infrastructure built outside government in a generation.
