Where it started

Twenty-six years. One question.

Jim Blackburn has been asking the same question since he was seven years old, watching animals love their young without thinking about it — without choosing it. Is love voluntary? Or does it just happen, automatic and unconditional, before thought?

That question — asked first in a college philosophy class at nineteen, asked again while reading the Bible cover to cover at forty, and asked one more time in the document that became TwoSoldiers.org — is the thread that runs through everything he builds.

Not a product. Not a platform. A reckoning.

A brief introduction

The man behind the mission.

Jim is a top-producing mortgage professional based in Fort Lauderdale, where he and his wife Olga also operate a real estate practice. He has spent more than twenty years helping families build wealth through homeownership — and the same disciplined, long-game mindset that built that career is what underwrites TwoSoldiers.org.

He is also a writer. He is currently completing a memoir titled My Journey Home, and a thirteen-book spiritual fiction series called The SonFlower and the Bear — both built around the same question that drives TwoSoldiers.

In the long run, Jim is preparing to run for Mayor of Fort Lauderdale on a platform built around the methodology he developed, called Character Branding® — the idea that public ventures should be anchored to a single, durable conviction rather than to shifting opinion.

TwoSoldiers.org is the conviction.

The question that wouldn't go away

"For what reason does a man who loves his family have to carry the weight of war for the rest of his life — alone?"

That is the question. It has been asked by every soldier who came home and never told anyone what happened. It has been asked by every wife who watched her husband sit silent in the dark. It has been asked by every son who never quite understood the look on his father's face.

For most of human history, there was no answer. The man who could give the answer was on the other side of the wire. In a different country. Speaking a different language. Carrying the same weight.

TwoSoldiers.org is the answer.
